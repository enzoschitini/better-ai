Acquarello is an AI image generation application, built in Streamlit, that puts the power of generative models in anyone's hands — no code, no hassle. Describe what you imagine and get an image; or upload an existing image and reinvent it. In both cases, you define the visual style right in the sidebar and watch the result take on your look.

✨ Highlights

  • 🖋️ Text → Image — describe a scene and let the AI create it.
  • 🖼️ Image → Image — start from an image and turn it into something new.
  • 🎚️ Style under control — choose and adjust the style from the sidebar.
  • ⚡ Simple to use — intuitive interface, designed for any user.

Context

Generative image models are among the most impressive advances in recent AI — but, in practice, they still intimidate non-technical people: they require configuration, complex prompts and scattered tools. Acquarello was born to solve this, bringing together text generation, image generation and style control in a single accessible interface. The proposal is to bring anyone closer to visual creation with AI — from the curious to the creative — showing that this technology can be powerful and simple at the same time.

Use cases

1. Generation from text (text-to-image)

A sentence becomes an image. Ideal for creating concepts, illustrations and scenes from scratch

"Gere uma imagem aquarela com cores bem vibrantes e coloridas de duas crianças brincando com uma pipa na calçada"

"Retrato de uma senhora idosa sorrindo, com rugas marcadas e olhar expressivo"

"Rua de vila japonesa à noite com lanternas acesas e chuva leve"

"Uma família reunida na sala assistindo a televisão nos anos 1950"

2. Generation from image (image-to-image)

One image turns into another. Perfect for reinterpreting, remixing or refining existing visuals.
